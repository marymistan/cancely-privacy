Privacy Policy

Cancely · Last updated: October 2026

Your data stays on your device

Cancely does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All your subscription data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.

Bank statements

When you import a bank statement CSV, the transaction data is sent to our secure API server solely to identify recurring subscriptions using AI. We do not store, log, or share your transaction data. The data is processed in real time and immediately discarded.

No account required

Cancely works without creating an account. We do not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable information.

No advertising

We do not use advertising SDKs, track your behavior, or sell your data to third parties. Cancely is completely ad-free.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Third-party services

Cancely uses Anthropic's Claude AI API to analyze bank transactions. Only the transaction text you submit is processed. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.

Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data from your device.

Contact
